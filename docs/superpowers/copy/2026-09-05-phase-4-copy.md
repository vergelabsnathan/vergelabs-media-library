# Phase 4, task 17 — the copy pass

The strings this pass changes, one row each. Nothing is edited until Nathan
approves the table. The rule each row fails is
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-05-folders-screen-design.md` §5 — every
sentence states a fact, or an action with its consequence; numbers where they
inform; no question the person did not ask; no pleasantry.

**What the pass found.** Less than the phase assumed. Phases 0 and 3 already
wrote the dashboard, the AI screen, Folders, Licence, Import and Duplicates to
the standard: every screen's title and its line beneath it are facts already,
and the audit turned up no "Let's", no "You are here", no smiley, no
exclamation on a screen. What is left is twenty-one strings, and almost all of
them are in the two places nobody re-read — the taxonomy and file-type
dialogs inherited from the plugin this was forked from, and three notes.

**Out of this phase.** Per-control help text (`core/help.php`'s `$texts`, some
two hundred entries) and the `confirm()` dialogs themselves. Spec §4.4 says
confirmation is the button, not a dialog; replacing those dialogs is behaviour
and belongs in its own task. Rows 8, 14 and 20 fix the wording of dialogs that
stay.
