## What changed in the runner (`tools/matrix.mjs`)

- `--parallel N`: the version and language cells through a pool of N (three here —
  2.5 GB free on this laptop); companions and box cells sequential. Pooled cells ran
  150–290 s against 130–200 s alone; eleven in 15 min instead of 37.
- Mock in every cell: `VERGEML_AI_MOCK` on every Playground; the box cells read
  `vergeml_ai` with WP-CLI (`{"site_profile":""}` on `/var/www/ms`), write it with
  `mock: 1`, put the snapshot back in the `finally` and read it back as the row's
  last step ("the site's vergeml_ai option is as it was — restored: …").
- The ms2 cell carries a `skip` reason; skips print — and are counted apart in the
  closing line; rows written by `--cell` carry `rerun: true` and render "(rerun)".
- Review patches (`9d59493`): the read-back step; "absent" is WP-CLI's "Does it
  exist?" only, anything else stops the cell before the write; a throw in one pooled
  cell is that cell's ✗, not the pool's end; whole-line output tags; `--parallel abc`
  → exit 2. `docs/testing.md`'s matrix paragraph and the submission doc's evidence
  line (`docs/wordpress-org-submission.md:39`, "18 of 18 on 09-11" → today's
  numbers) follow the run.
