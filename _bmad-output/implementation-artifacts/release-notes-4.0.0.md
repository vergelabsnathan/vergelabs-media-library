**The Folders screen is a workflow, not a conversation.** Tree, Fill, Describe, Alt text, Rename — each step reachable by clicking, none of them a gate. Paste your folder tree one folder per line, or build it by clicking. Confirm locks the tree; the words a folder files by are yours to edit.

**Filing reads what a picture shows.** One way of filing with three honest answers: it fits, it is between two folders, or nothing fits. A picture you placed by hand is never moved. The estimate before you press is the number that happens. Why a picture is where it is now shows on its own edit screen and in the grid.

**A shop sorts itself.** One press turns your product categories into folders, and pictures are filed by the product they belong to before anything is guessed at — on a real shop that placed 32 of 33.

**The media library at a quarter of a million pictures.** The smart-folder counts went from ten seconds of database time on every admin page to 8.6 milliseconds. The list toolbar is one row; the folders panel is on the list view too.
