### Aggregate views

**A2 — Four ssh wrappers, three `judge` copies (duplication, pre-existing plus one).** `plugin/tools/verify.mjs` (`boxStep`, `runPhp`), `pro/tools/verify.mjs` (`runBox`), `tools/box-eval.mjs`, and the epic's new `tools/upg-pro-shots.mjs` (`box`, `wpEval`) each spawn ssh with the same key args; `countedChecks`/`judge` exist in both runners (`plugin/tools/verify.mjs:646-659, 724-737`; `pro/tools/verify.mjs:95-109`), and two of the three never require `N === M`, relying on the suite's own `exit(1)` surviving the transport. ### Diff-scope review (`bmad-review`, three lenses)

- **R4 (adversarial, edge-case) — the 1.2 suite's `debug.log` window is open-ended.** `upgrade-3161.php:107-122` judges every line after the snapshot's count (54) on a fixture that stays up; today the file is 1,154 lines and the suite is **31/31** (1,100 new lines, 144 known).
