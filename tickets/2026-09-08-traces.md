# INITIAL — The plugin, held to our own white paper

Asked by Nathan on 2026-09-08: apply the principles in `What does this
conclusion rest on` v6.3 EN to this plugin's AI build and architecture,
without disturbing normal flow and automation, and put it through the harness.

The diagnosis is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-08-traces-diagnosis.md`, the
spec is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-08-traces.md`, the plan is
`plans/traces.md`. Nothing is built. The stop point is Nathan's approval of the
mock named in the spec.

## FEATURE

**The reason a picture is where it is, kept.** `vergeml_filing_pick()` already
computes `why`, `score`, `runner_up`, `runner_score` and the full score map for
every picture it places or refuses to place, and every one of those values is
discarded the moment the function returns. They are written to the move that
acted on them, together with the `prompt_hash` and `model_version` of the
description they were computed from. An abstention is written too, as a row
with `term_id = 0`, because "the evidence would not place this" is the answer
to the question nobody can currently ask.

**A model that does not count.** The guide's conversation proposes folders and
reports nothing. The numbers an owner reads come from a dry run of the matcher
the plugin already has, per folder, plus one line for what the run would leave
alone. The prompt gains one rule: speak about the draft, never about the
library, and give no number of your own.

**A record of who approved what.** `vergeml_librarian_batches` gains `user_id`
and `approved_at`. A filed draft names the person and the moment.

**No fabricated zeros.** A failed request says it failed instead of rendering
`{ rules: [], unfiled: 0, pictures: 0 }` as fact beside a destructive control.

## WHY

We publish a white paper that asks vendors to produce the log of a random
finding, to show what a claim rests on, and to say who approved which version.
Four of its six layers already hold here, and `core/filing.php`'s abstain rule
is a better answer to the Retrieval layer than most of the systems the paper
was written about. Three do not hold, and the largest is the one the paper is
entirely about: the connection between the evidence and the decision is
computed and thrown away.

The customer-visible half is smaller and immediate. On 4 September the guide
told Nathan *"all 641 images are now routed"* and, asked directly, *"did you
put them in folder yes or no?"*, answered *"Yes"*. Nothing had happened; the
library had 112 pictures in no folder. The counts it quoted were generated by a
language model that had no way to compute them.

## OUT OF SCOPE

- Any change to what the matcher decides. The floor stays 0.55, the margin
  0.08, the gates as they are. The plan's last gate is that the same pass over
  the same pictures produces identical placements before and after.
- The describe pipeline, the scheduler, auto-file, the watch, the tree, the
  Move button. Behaviour unchanged.
- Backfilling history. An empty `why` means "before this shipped".
- The Storage layer. That is the service's, and it is answered on the site.
- Alt-per-placement and decorative detection. A separate phase, unrelated.

## OTHER CONSIDERATIONS

- **The dashboard says 116 and the Folders screen says 112.** Both are right —
  645 attachments, 641 images, and `vergeml_folders_facts()` only ever counts
  described pictures. Two populations, one phrase. Fixing the labels is a copy
  change and is not in this ticket; it is on the list.
- **This ships after the listing.** Nothing here blocks submitting the free
  plugin, and it should not delay it.
- The counts in the guide are the visible symptom; the discarded `why` is the
  architecture. Fixing only the first would leave the product unable to answer
  its own white paper's question.
